        We are always looking for passionate and curious researchers to join the Marquardt Lab. In case you have a passion for RNA Biology, Gene Regulation and Epigenetics research please [get in touch](../contact/) to explore options. Molecular Genetics research in particular is often great fun!

        **Independent Fellowships**

        Independent Fellowships are a fantastic way to join the lab. We offer full support in drafting the idea, writing the proposal, and with mock interviews if necessary. The project should be related to our research interests to maximise synergies. Some relevant fellowship schemes include:

        - [EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.embo.org/funding/fellowships-grants-and-career-support/postdoctoral-fellowships/)
        - [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/)
        - [DAAD Fellowship](https://www.daad.de/en/)
        - [Alexander von Humboldt Fellowship](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/)
        - [CAPES-Humboldt Research Fellowship](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/capes-humboldt-research-fellowship) *(for Brazilian scientists)*

        **PhD Students**

        We welcome applications from prospective PhD students. Please [get in touch](../contact/) with a brief description of your background and interests. For B.Sc. and M.Sc. student projects, see our [Student Projects](../student-projects/) page.
